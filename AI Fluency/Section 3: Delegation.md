### Section 3: Delegation — Kaam Ko Insaan Aur AI Ke Darmiyan Kaise Baantein

**Simple Explanation**

Sabse common beginner mistake **pehle prompt se pehle** hoti hai. Log AI assistant khol kar seedha typing shuru kar detay hain, bina pehle yeh decide kiye keh: Main asal mein kya achieve karna chahta hoon? Ek acha result kaisa dikhega? Kaunse hisse AI ko karne chahiyen? Kaunse hisse mujhe khud karne chahiyen? Kaunse decisions kabhi bhi AI ko nahi dene chahiyen? Yeh sab **Delegation** ka masla hai.

**Delegation ka matlab hai yeh decide karna keh kaam insaan aur AI ke darmiyan kaise baanta jaye.** Yeh sirf "AI ko kaam de dena" nahi hai — yeh poore workflow ko design karna hai.

Delegation ke teen hissay hain: **Problem awareness** (goal aur kaam ko samajhna), **Platform awareness** (samajhna keh kaunsa AI tool is kaam ke liye sahi hai), aur **Task delegation** (yeh decide karna keh har hissa kaun karega).

Ek example lo: farz karo tum chahtay ho AI ek agent banaye jo overdue invoices (jin bills ka payment late ho) chase karay. Ek beginner sirf itna likhega: "mujhay invoice-chasing agent bana do." Lekin asal mushkil sawal abhi bhi baqi hain: Kaunse customers ko contact karna hai? Kitne din late honay per invoice per action lena hai? Kaunsa tone use karna hai? Kitni amount per human approval chahiye? Agar customer invoice se disagree karay to kya hoga? Yeh sab prompting ke sawal nahi hain — yeh **business decisions** hain, jo AI khud nahi le sakta jab tak tum khud jaan boojh kar usay yeh authority na do.

Platform awareness ka matlab hai yeh samajhna keh har AI tool har kaam ke liye equally acha nahi hota — kabhi reasoning model chahiye mushkil multi-step problem ke liye, kabhi search-enabled assistant chahiye current information ke liye, kabhi coding agent chahiye software ke liye. Task delegation ka matlab hai kaam ko deliberately (jaan boojh kar) baantna — jaisay course banate waqt: audience aur learning goals decide karna insaan ka kaam hai (kyunke isay purpose aur judgment chahiye), pehla draft likhna AI ka kaam ho sakta hai (kyunke woh fast hai), lekin final approval hamesha insaan ka hi kaam hai (kyunke tumhara naam aur reputation us se juri hoti hai).

**Important Points**
* Delegation = decide karna keh insaan aur AI ke darmiyan kaam kaise baanta jaye — yeh workflow design hai, sirf task offload karna nahi.
* Teen hissay: **Problem awareness**, **Platform awareness**, aur **Task delegation**.
* Sahi sawal yeh nahi hai "kya AI yeh kar sakta hai?" balkay yeh hai: "kaunsa hissa AI karay, kaunsa main karoon, aur kyun?"
* Business policy ke decisions (jaisay kis customer ko kab contact karna hai) AI khud nahi le sakta, jab tak deliberately usay woh authority na di jaye.
* "Domain expert pehle, AI delegator baad mein" — matlab pehle apne field mein expertise honi chahiye, phir AI ko sahi tareeqay se delegate karna aata hai.

**Difficult Words**
* Delegation → Kaam ko decide karke insaan aur AI ke darmiyan baantna.
* Workflow → Kaam karne ka poora tareeqa/process, step by step.
* Domain expert → Apne field (jaisay accounting, teaching) ka mahir insaan.
* Escalate → Kisi masle ko upar wale (insaan) tak pohanchana jab AI khud decide na kar sakay.

**Quick Revision**
Delegation ka matlab hai kaam shuru karne se pehle yeh decide karna keh AI kya karay aur insaan kya karay. Iske teen hissay hain: Problem awareness (goal samajhna), Platform awareness (sahi tool chunna), aur Task delegation (kaam baantna). Business-level decisions AI ko nahi diye jate jab tak jaan boojh kar na diye jayein. Yaad rakho: sawal "kya AI yeh kar sakta hai" nahi, balkay "kis hissay ko AI karay aur kyun" hona chahiye.
