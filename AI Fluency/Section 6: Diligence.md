### Section 6: Diligence — AI Ko Responsibly Use Karo Aur Result Ki Zimmedari Lo

**Simple Explanation**

Pehle teen Ds tumhein behtar results dilatay hain. Diligence ek bilkul alag sawal poochti hai: **"Kya mujhe AI is tareeqay se use karna bhi chahiye?"**

Ek example lo: farz karo ek lecturer AI se students ke liye end-of-term feedback likhwata hai. Likhawat bohat achi hai. Lekin lecturer ne student names, grades, aur disciplinary notes ek consumer AI service mein paste kar diye jise university ne approve nahi kiya tha. Students ko yeh bhi nahi bataya gaya keh AI ne unke academic record ka hissa banne wale comments likhne mein madad ki. Output acha ho sakta hai — lekin AI ka istemaal phir bhi **irresponsible** hai.

**Diligence ka matlab hai AI ke istemaal aur uske result ki zimmedari lena.** Iske teen hissay hain: **Creation diligence** (banane se pehle aur banate waqt zimmedar choices lena), **Transparency diligence** (jab logon ko janna zaroori ho to AI ke role ke baray mein honest hona), aur **Deployment diligence** (kaam use ya release honay se pehle verify karna aur uski zimmedari lena).

**Creation diligence** mein tum pehle poochtay ho: kya is data mein personal information hai? Kya company ka confidential data hai? Kya mujhe yeh information is tool mein daalne ki ijazat hai? Data ko access kaun kar sakta hai? Asaan raasta hamesha responsible raasta nahi hota — real customer database ko kisi bhi khulay AI tool mein daal dena 5 minute bacha sakta hai lekin serious privacy problem bana sakta hai. Aksar hal yeh hota hai keh kaam chhorne ke bajaye data ko **redact** (chhupana/hatana) kar do — jaisay us lecturer ke case mein, names aur student IDs hata kar sirf grade range aur ek behavior rakh kar feedback likhwaya ja sakta tha. Redaction ka test yeh hai: kya sirf yeh dekh kar koi pehchan sakta hai keh yeh kiske baray mein hai?

**Transparency diligence** mein yeh dekha jata hai keh har AI-assisted kaam ke liye elaan zaroori nahi, lekin jab AI doosron per material asar dalta hai (jaisay academic kaam, hiring decisions, customer communications, medical/financial advice), transparency zaroori ho jati hai. Usool yeh hai: **jitna zyada AI ka natija dosron per asar dalta hai, transparency utni hi zyada zaroori hoti hai.**

**Deployment diligence** mein AI-assisted kaam publish/send/execute honay se pehle check kiya jata hai — facts verify karna, sources confirm karna, calculations check karna, bias check karna, aur high-impact actions ke liye human approval lena. Ek important qaida hai: **koi bhi number jis per decision khara hai (total, percentage, headcount) hamesha calculate kiya jana chahiye, kabhi bhi AI se sirf generate nahi karwana chahiye** — kyunke AI number ko "predict" karta hai jaisay ek likely-lagne wala total, jo galat ho sakta hai chahe har line item sahi ho.

Sabse powerful sawal jo tum apne aap se poochh saktay ho: **"kya main confidently apna naam is per laga sakta hoon?"** Agar jawab "nahi" hai, to kaam abhi ready nahi hai. Aur agar jawab clear nahi hai, to guess karke aagay barhne ke bajaye, us insaan tak baat le jao jo us decision ka zimmedar hai. Sabse zaroori sabaq: **AI kaam automate kar sakta hai, lekin zimmedari (accountability) automate nahi kar sakta.** Agar AI-assisted system koi nuqsan dena wala decision leta hai, tab bhi zimmedari organization aur us insaan per hi rehti hai jisne isay use kiya.

**Important Points**
* Diligence = AI ke istemaal aur uske result ki zimmedari lena.
* Teen hissay: **Creation diligence** (banane se pehle), **Transparency diligence** (istemaal ke doraan honest hona), **Deployment diligence** (release se pehle verify karna).
* Redaction ka test: kya sirf diya gaya data se koi pehchan sakta hai keh yeh kiske baray mein hai?
* Usool: jitna zyada asar dosron per, utni zyada transparency zaroori.
* "The numbers rule": important numbers hamesha calculate kiye jayein, sirf AI se generate na karwaye jayein.
* Powerful sawal: "kya main is per confidently apna naam laga sakta hoon?"
* AI kaam automate kar sakta hai, lekin **accountability (zimmedari) automate nahi kar sakta.**

**Difficult Words**
* Diligence → AI ke istemaal aur uske natijay ki zimmedari lena.
* Redaction → Data se woh tafseelat hatana jo kisi insaan ki pehchan zaahir karti hon.
* Transparency → Honesty aur khulapan, khaas kar AI ke role ke baray mein.
* Accountability → Kisi kaam ke natijay ki zimmedari lena.

**Quick Revision**
Diligence yeh poochti hai keh kya AI ko is tareeqay se use karna bhi chahiye — sirf achha output kaafi nahi. Iske teen hissay hain: Creation, Transparency, aur Deployment diligence. Sensitive data ko redact kiya ja sakta hai taake kaam bhi ho jaye aur privacy bhi mehfooz rahe. Important numbers hamesha calculate kiye jayein, AI se generate nahi karwaye jayein. Aur sabse bara sabaq: AI kaam kar sakta hai, lekin zimmedari hamesha insaan per rehti hai.
