### Section 8: Common Mistakes, Checklist, aur Recap

**Simple Explanation**

Zyadatar frustrating AI experiences inhi chaar mistakes mein se kisi ek ki wajah se hoti hain.

**Mistake 1: Problem define karne se pehle prompt likhna.** Log seedha typing shuru kar detay hain bina yeh decide kiye keh success kaisi dikhegi. Yahan missing skill hai **Delegation**. Iska hal: pehle goal, audience, constraints, aur human/AI split decide karo.

**Mistake 2: Pehle jawab ko hi final jawab samajh lena.** Log assume kar letay hain keh agar pehla response weak hai to AI bekar hai. Yahan missing skill hai **Description + Discernment loop**. Hal: result ko inspect karo, specific feedback do, aur dobara try karo.

**Mistake 3: Polished jawab ko professional lagne ki wajah se trust kar lena.** Log fluency ko accuracy samajh baithtay hain. Yahan missing skill hai **Discernment**. Hal: important facts, assumptions, calculations, aur sources ko verify karo.

**Mistake 4: Privacy ya accountability ke baray mein sirf tab sochna jab kuch ghalat ho jaye.** Log sirf kaam khatam karne per focus karte hain aur yeh nahi sochtay keh AI kaise use ho raha hai. Yahan missing skill hai **Diligence**. Hal: data, disclosure, approval, aur accountability ke rules deployment se pehle hi decide karo.

Ek roz-marra (daily) istemaal ke liye chaar sawalon ka **checklist** hai jo kisi bhi meaningful AI kaam se pehle poochha ja sakta hai: **Delegate** — goal kya hai? AI kya karay? kya mere paas rahay? **Describe** — output, context, method, aur behavior mein se AI ko kya chahiye? **Discern** — mujhe kaise pata chalega keh jawab sahi, mukammal, aur useful hai? **Be diligent** — kya data safe hai? kya AI ke role ko disclose karna zaroori hai? result ka approval/ownership kis ke paas hai?

Aakhir mein poore course ka **short recap**: AI fluency ka matlab prompts yaad rakhna nahi hai — yeh AI ke sath **effectively, efficiently, ethically, aur safely** kaam karne ki salahiyat hai. Teen modes hain: Automation, Augmentation, Agency. Chaaron competencies hamesha same rehti hain: **Delegation** (human/AI split decide karo), **Description** (AI ko wo do jo chahiye), **Discernment** (jo wapas aaye usay judge karo), **Diligence** (responsibly use karo aur outcome ki zimmedari lo). Agar sirf ek jumla yaad rakhna ho: **"Decide what AI should do. Describe the work clearly. Check what comes back. Own what happens next."**

**Important Points**
* 4 common mistakes: (1) Problem define kiye bagair prompt likhna → missing Delegation, (2) pehle jawab ko final samajhna → missing Description+Discernment loop, (3) polish ko accuracy samajhna → missing Discernment, (4) sirf kaam khatam karne per focus, privacy/accountability baad mein sochna → missing Diligence.
* Daily checklist: Delegate, Describe, Discern, Be Diligent — har ek ke liye apna sawal.
* Final one-liner yaad rakhne wala: **"Decide → Describe → Check → Own."**
* AI fluency = Effective + Efficient + Ethical + Safe kaam karna.
* Yeh checklist "har chhoti task ke liye paperwork" banane ke liye nahi hai — maqsad hai keh yeh chaar sawal automatic ho jayein.

**Difficult Words**
* Checklist → Poochne ke liye sawalon ki fehrist jo kisi kaam se pehle dekhi jaye.
* Recap → Kisi topic ka mukhtasar (short) dobara jaiza.
* Outcome → Kisi kaam ka natija (result).

**Quick Revision**
Chaar common beginner mistakes hain, aur har ek kisi na kisi D ki kami se juri hai — Delegation, Description+Discernment loop, Discernment, aur Diligence. Ek simple daily checklist hai: Delegate, Describe, Discern, Be Diligent — yeh chaar sawal har baar poochne se AI ke sath kaam karna zyada effective ho jata hai. Poore course ka core message: AI fluency ka matlab hai good decisions lena AI ke sath — Decide, Describe, Check, aur Own.
